📚 Understanding the Metrics

Trustworthiness: Measures whether points that appear close in the embedding were actually close in the original space (avoids false neighbors).

Continuity: Measures whether points that were close in the original space remain close in the embedding (preserves true neighbors).

Ideal scores: Both metrics range from 0 to 1, with 1 being perfect. Values above 0.9 are excellent.
