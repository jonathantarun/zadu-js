Metric Interpretation Guide

Metric What it measures Low score means
Trustworthiness Are nearby points in the embedding actually nearby in the original space? Detects false neighbors — points that appear close in 2D but were far apart in high-D. The embedding introduces misleading proximity between unrelated points.
Continuity Are nearby points in the original space still nearby in the embedding? Detects missing neighbors — points that were close in high-D but got separated in 2D. The embedding tears apart local neighborhoods that existed in the original data.
Steadiness Do clusters visible in the embedding correspond to real groups in the original space? Detects false clusters created by the projection. The embedding shows groups that don't actually exist — artifacts of the projection.
Cohesiveness Do real clusters from the original space remain intact in the embedding? Detects split clusters torn apart by the projection. Real groups in the data are fragmented or scattered across the embedding.
Key trade-off: Trustworthiness and Continuity often conflict — improving one can degrade the other. Similarly, Steadiness and Cohesiveness represent opposing views of cluster reliability. The goal is to find a parameter balance where all metrics are acceptable, not to maximize any single one.
Score interpretation: excellent ≥ 0.90   good ≥ 0.85   fair ≥ 0.80   poor < 0.80
